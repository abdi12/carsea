# Home page

Scope: `/[lang]` home, plus the shared header, footer, wordmark, tokens, and primitives it introduces. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: an everyday buyer in Greater Jakarta, usually on a phone, deciding whether Carsea is worth starting their car research on. Action: start the guided finder from its first question, or go to browse. Proof available: none beyond the method itself; no catalog, photography, or social proof exists, so the page demonstrates how a Carsea fact is recorded using clearly labelled illustrative cells.

Constraints: Bahasa Indonesia default with English equivalent; light theme default with full dark theme; glass only on the header; nothing may resemble paid promotion.

Memorable moment: answering the first finder question presses a violet stamp into the cell.

Unresolved: finder budget brackets are placeholders until Phase 4; pages behind the entry paths are noindex "being built" stubs.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Carsea is a stamped service booklet for a decision. Every fact is a ruled cell that carries its own source stamp and date. It refuses the car-site default of a glossy hero photo over a search bar and a grid of car cards.

OWN-WORLD: A deep green vinyl cover field (#0E5C3F) owning whole regions; cool green-white booklet pages (#F4F7F3) ruled into square-cornered cells with hairline rules; near-black green ink (#12201A); one violet rubber-stamp ink (#5B3FD1) reserved for verification and selection. One variable grotesque at two extremes: expanded heavy for titles and the wordmark, condensed bold caps inside stamps and cell labels. Stamps are round or lozenge outlines set a few degrees off-axis. Everything locks to one cell module.

STORY: The visitor understands Carsea is independent research for new cars, believes it because facts visibly carry sources and nothing is sponsored, and starts the finder or browses.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A glass header (wordmark, primary links, language, theme) over the green cover. The cover carries the offer as one expanded headline at up to 6rem and a single supporting line. Overlapping the cover's lower edge, the first booklet page: the finder's first question, budget, as a row of selectable cells with a skip cell, and the primary action "continue" at its right end. Browse cars sits directly under it as a second ruled entry row.

FORM: The stamped service booklet (buku servis); position 5 of 7 on the ordered grounded list; seed key 73a13bb6. Signature interaction: the stamp press, a 180ms press-in of the violet stamp when a cell is filled, static under reduced motion. Motion grammar: press-in only; nothing loops, nothing follows scroll. Raises: color at region scale; nothing off the cell module; ordered stations with review before match.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
